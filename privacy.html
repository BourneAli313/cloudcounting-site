Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and do your books.
  • We never sell your information or share it for marketing.
  • You invite us into your own QuickBooks Online file. Your books stay yours.
  • Some tools we use store data outside Canada. We list them below and confirm them in writing before we start.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

CloudCounting ("we", "us") is a cloud-based bookkeeping firm serving small businesses across Canada. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit cloudcounting.ca, contact us, or become a client. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where they apply, provincial privacy laws such as British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act.

What we collect

When you contact us

Through our contact form or by email: your name, company name, email address, phone number, province, industry, accounting software, approximate transaction volume, how you heard about us, and anything you choose to tell us in your message.

When you become a client

The financial records needed to keep your books: bank and credit card transactions, invoices, bills, receipts, payroll summaries and sales tax filings, and contact details for the people we work with at your business. We try not to collect Social Insurance Numbers or full bank account numbers, and we remove them from files we receive wherever we can.

When you visit the website

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they use. It records information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and the pages you view. It does not tell us who you are. You can opt out with your browser's privacy settings or Google's opt-out add-on.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To provide bookkeeping services under your engagement letter.
  • To send you service messages, such as requests for receipts or notices that your month is closed.
  • To meet our legal and professional record-keeping obligations.
  • To improve our website, using analytics that do not identify you.

We do not use your information for any other purpose without asking you first. We do not sell it or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Consent

By sending us the contact form you agree that we may contact you about your enquiry. By signing an engagement letter you agree to the handling of your financial information described here and in that letter. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us, although we may then be unable to continue providing services, and we may still need to keep some records to meet legal requirements.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers to run our business. Each of them only receives what it needs to do its job.

  • Intuit QuickBooks Online stores your books. You own the QuickBooks file and invite us in as your accountant user.
  • FormSubmit delivers messages sent through our website contact form to our inbox.
  • Our email and file-storage providers hold correspondence and documents you send us.
  • Google Analytics provides website statistics.
  • Software that helps us sort and check transactions, including AI tools. A person reviews every month's work and is responsible for the result.

Some of these providers store or process data in the United States or other countries, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. Before we start working together, we tell you in writing which tools we use for your books and where they process data.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid request from the Canada Revenue Agency or a court order.

How we protect it

  • We never ask for or keep your banking or QuickBooks passwords.
  • Every account we use for client work is protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Access to client information is limited to the people working on your books.
  • We avoid handling SINs and full account numbers wherever we can.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach involving your personal information creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 12 months. For clients, we keep working papers and correspondence for as long as the law and our professional obligations require, generally six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, which matches the Canada Revenue Agency's record-keeping period. Your QuickBooks Online file is yours and remains under your control after our engagement ends.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we have used it,
  • correct anything that is inaccurate, or
  • delete information we are not required to keep.

Email info@CloudCounting.ca and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in British Columbia, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and its effective date. Clients will be told about significant changes by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to our privacy contact at info@CloudCounting.ca.
