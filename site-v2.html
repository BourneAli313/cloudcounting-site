Your month with us

Last month’s books, closed this month

Here is what a typical close looks like. Timing depends on how quickly we get what we need, but your books are always closed the following month.

This month we close last month’s books

  1. As the month begins

    We pull your feeds

    Bank and card transactions for the month come into QuickBooks Online, and we sort what is already clear.

  2. Early in the month

    One short list of questions

    You get a single list of anything we need, such as a missing receipt or an unfamiliar payment. No back-and-forth emails, and the sooner we hear back, the sooner your month closes.

  3. Once we have what we need

    We reconcile and check taxes

    Every account is reconciled and your GST/HST and PST are checked against the month's sales and purchases.

    Software helps us move fast. A person checks every month before anything reaches you.

  5. Within the following month

    Your month is closed

    Every account is reconciled in QuickBooks Online, so your profit and loss and balance sheet for that month are ready to pull once it is closed.

Security and privacy

Your financial data stays protected

We handle sensitive information every day, so we keep the rules simple and tell you exactly how it works.

  • No shared passwords

    You invite us to QuickBooks Online as your accountant user. We never hold your login.

  • Multi-factor sign-in

    Every account we use for your books is protected with multi-factor authentication.

  • Only what we need

    We avoid handling SINs and full account numbers, and we remove them from files wherever we can.

  • Canadian privacy rules

    We handle your information with the federal PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws in mind and explain it in our engagement letter.

  • AI with a human reviewer

    Software helps sort and check transactions. A person reviews every month and is accountable for the result.

  • Clear on where data goes

    Some tools may process data outside Canada. We tell you which ones, in writing, before we start.

FAQ

Questions owners ask first

How is my financial data protected?

You invite us into QuickBooks Online as your accountant user, so we never hold your passwords. We use multi-factor authentication and limit the personal information we handle. Some tools we use may process data outside Canada, and we explain this in our engagement letter.

Do you use AI?

Yes. Software helps us sort and check transactions quickly. A person reviews every month before anything reaches you, and we are responsible for the result.

Can you take over from my current bookkeeper?

Yes. We review your last few months, tidy up what needs fixing and keep your history intact.

Do you file my taxes?

We prepare GST/HST and PST returns and hand your clean books to your accountant for year-end. Bookkeeping is not audit, assurance or legal advice.

What if my transaction volume changes?

We review volume each quarter and adjust your fixed fee if your business grows. A small per-transaction rate covers occasional busy months.
