Frequently Asked Questions

What accounting software do you use? ▼
We specialize in QuickBooks Online, Sage, and Xero. We can work with other platforms and can help migrate your data if needed.
How quickly can you get started? ▼
Most clients are onboarded within 1-2 weeks. We'll schedule a consultation first to understand your needs and timeline.
Do you file taxes on behalf of clients? ▼
We prepare your financial statements and reconciled books. Tax filing is typically done by a CPA or accountant, but we provide all the data you'll need.
How is my data kept secure? ▼
All client data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security protocols and never store sensitive credentials.
What if I need to catch up on years of bookkeeping? ▼
We offer catch-up bookkeeping services. We'll organize and reconcile historical transactions to get your books current, then move to ongoing monthly service.
